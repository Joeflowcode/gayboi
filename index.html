Before you book

Straight answers.

If your question isn’t here, call or text. A photo usually makes it easy to answer.

How is junk removal priced?+

Most jobs are based on the amount of truck space used, plus factors like unusually heavy material or difficult access. We confirm your price before loading begins.

Can you pick up this week?+

Same-day and next-day openings are often available, depending on the route and job size. Text early in the day for the quickest scheduling answer.

Do I need to move everything outside?+

No. Show us what goes and we’ll carry it out from the garage, upstairs, backyard, storage area, or wherever it sits.

What can’t you haul?+

We generally cannot take wet paint, chemicals, fuel, asbestos, or other hazardous materials. Send a photo if you’re unsure about a particular item.

What happens to reusable items?+

Loads are sorted for donation and recycling when practical, with the remainder taken to the appropriate disposal facility.
