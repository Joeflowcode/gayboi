Good To Know

Frequently asked questions

Still curious? Give us a call — we're happy to help.

How much does junk removal cost?

Pricing is based on how much space your items take up in our truck. Every job starts with a free, no-obligation estimate, and the price we quote is the price you pay — no hidden fees and no surprises at the end.

Do you offer same-day service?

Often, yes! We're open Monday–Saturday from 7am to 7pm with same-day slots frequently available, plus Sunday appointments by request. Call us early for the best chance at a same-day pickup.

What happens to my junk after pickup?

We divert about 70% of everything we collect away from the landfill. Usable items are donated to local charities and the rest is sorted for recycling wherever possible. Only what truly can't be reused is disposed of.

Do I need to move everything outside first?

Not at all. Just point to what you want gone and our crew does all the lifting and loading — from the attic, basement, backyard, or anywhere on your property.

What items won't you take?

For safety and legal reasons we generally can't take hazardous materials like wet paint, chemicals, asbestos, or fuels. If you're unsure about a specific item, just ask — we'll point you in the right direction.
