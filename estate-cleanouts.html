Estate Sale & Cleanout FAQs

Do we need to sort the entire house before contacting you?

No. Secure important personal items, documents, medications, firearms and anything that absolutely must stay, then contact us before spending weeks sorting everything else.

Does every property qualify for a full estate sale?

No. Some properties make more sense as a partial sale, direct cleanout or another liquidation approach. The free evaluation is designed to help determine the best fit.

Do you charge a commission?

Estate sales are typically priced on a commission basis with the exact rate agreed in writing before work begins. The rate can vary with the size, labor, setup and expected sale.

Is the final cleanout included in the commission?

Not automatically. Disposal, donation hauling and post-sale cleanout are quoted separately so the family can see what the remaining work actually costs.

Can you help after another estate sale company finishes?

Yes. Final haul-off and cleanout work can be quoted separately after another company or family-run sale.

What areas do you serve?

Our primary area includes Salem, Turner, Aumsville, Stayton, Sublimity, Lyons, Mehama, Mill City, Gates, Detroit, Idanha and surrounding Santiam Canyon communities.
