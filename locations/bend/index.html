Good To Know

Junk removal in Bend — FAQs

Still curious? Give us a call — we're happy to help.

Do you offer same-day junk removal in Bend?

Often, yes! We're open Monday–Saturday from 7am to 7pm with same-day slots frequently available in Bend, plus Sunday appointments by request. Call early for the best shot at a same-day pickup.

How much does junk removal cost in Bend?

Pricing is based on how much space your items take up in our truck. Every Bend job starts with a free, no-obligation estimate, and the price we quote is the price you pay — no hidden fees.

What happens to my junk after pickup?

We divert about 70% of everything we collect away from the landfill. Usable items go to local charities and the rest is sorted for recycling wherever possible.

Do I need to move everything outside first?

Not at all. Just point to what you want gone and our crew does all the lifting and loading — from the attic, basement, backyard, or anywhere on your Bend property.

What items won't you take?

For safety and legal reasons we generally can't take hazardous materials like wet paint, chemicals, asbestos, or fuels. If you're unsure about a specific item, just ask.
